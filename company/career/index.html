How hiring works

Four conversations, no take-home marathon

We keep the process short and tell you where you stand after every step.

1 · Apply

A CV is enough. A cover letter is welcome but never required.

2 · First call

Thirty minutes with the hiring manager about the role and your work.

3 · Meet the team

A working session with the people you would sit next to.

4 · Offer

A decision within a week, and honest feedback either way.
