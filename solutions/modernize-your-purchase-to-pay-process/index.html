Three of them close.
Here is each one.

  1. AP teamApprover

    Re-keying what your supplier already typed

    The handover today

    • The invoice arrives as a PDF. Someone opens it and types the header, the lines and the VAT into Business Central.
    • Someone finds the purchase order, opens the receipt, and compares three documents on one screen.
    • The invoice is emailed to an approver who is travelling, and it waits there until they are back at a desk.

    What takes it over

    Continia Document Capture

    Reads the invoice and creates the purchase document in Business Central, with the lines already on it.

    • Header, lines and VAT recognized from the PDF or the e-document — no typing.
    • Three-way matching against the order and the receipt, so variances are what your team looks at.
    • Approval happens in Business Central or on a phone, with the audit trail attached to the document.
    Data CaptureOrder MatchingApproval WorkflowSecure Archive

  2. ApproverBank

    Walking the payment over to the bank

    The handover today

    • Approved invoices are collected into a payment file, exported, and uploaded through the bank's portal.
    • Account details are checked against an email, or against a vendor record nobody has updated this year.
    • Two people log into two banks with two different tokens, and the file format is different in each.

    What takes it over

    Payment Management & Continia Banking

    Builds the payment from the open entries already in Business Central and sends it to the bank over a direct connection.

    • Payment proposals come from the ledger itself, not from a spreadsheet built beside it.
    • Vendor bank details are validated before the payment leaves.
    • One workflow across several banks, so no file is carried by hand to a portal.
    Payment ProposalsVendor ValidationDirect Bank ConnectionMulti-bank

  3. BankYour ledger

    Matching the statement back to the ledger

    The handover today

    • The statement is downloaded and read against open entries, one line at a time.
    • Partial payments, fees and currency differences are resolved by hand, at the end of the month.
    • The close starts late because reconciliation starts late, and the two are the same queue.

    What takes it over

    Continia Banking

    Brings the statement in on its own and reconciles it against the open entries it already knows about.

    • Statements import automatically — nobody downloads a file to start the day.
    • What matches is posted; what does not is the short list a person actually reads.
    • Bank and Business Central stay in sync through the month, not on the last day of it.
    Statement ImportAutomatic ReconciliationMulti-bank
