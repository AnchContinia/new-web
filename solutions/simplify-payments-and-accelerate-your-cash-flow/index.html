Four steps from proposal to reconciled.

Every payment run follows the same automated path — you only step in when a check needs a human decision.

1 · Propose

Business Central builds the payment proposal from due invoices — no manual entry.

2 · Validate

Amounts, IBANs and payee details are checked and fraud-screened before approval.

3 · Send

Approved payments go straight to the bank as a secure file — no portal login.

4 · Reconcile

Statements import automatically and match themselves — up to 95% hands-free.
