Continia opens a new office to serve the DACH region

Closer local presence for German-speaking markets, where e-invoicing requirements and Business Central adoption are both moving quickly.

Newsroom11 Mar 2026
Prototype copy

This newsroom entry is placeholder content for the site prototype. Official Continia announcements are published at continia.com/news.

Continia has opened a new office serving the DACH region.

The move follows demand from German-speaking markets, where regulatory change around electronic invoicing and steady Business Central adoption are pushing finance teams to automate document handling sooner than planned.

The office supports the local partner network rather than replacing it — implementation and day-to-day support stay with the partners who already work alongside customers' finance teams.

About Continia

Continia builds finance-automation apps that run inside Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central — document capture, expense management, payments, outgoing documents and e-invoicing. Every app installs from Microsoft AppSource and updates with the customer's Business Central release, so there is no separate system to integrate or maintain.

With more than 30 years behind it, Continia serves over 21,000 customers worldwide, delivered and supported through a network of 1,500+ local Business Central partners in 45+ countries.
