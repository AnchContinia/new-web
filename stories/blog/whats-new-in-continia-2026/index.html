What's new in Continia 2026: smarter matching, faster posting

A tour of the modules finance teams lean on most — what each one actually removes from the working day, and where to look when you want the detail.

Product team18 Jun 20264 min read

The most useful way to read a release is not feature by feature, but by asking what leaves the working day. Here is that view across the modules finance teams touch most.

Looking for the official release notes?

This is an overview of what the modules do. Version-specific release details, availability dates and supported Business Central versions live on continia.com — always check there before planning an upgrade.

Capture: fewer fields, fewer people

Intelligent OCR reads incoming invoices and drops recognised values straight into the right Business Central fields. The gain is not only typing time — it is the class of error that comes from typing at all: transposed digits, the wrong vendor when two names are similar, a date entered in the previous period.

Matching: three-way, with tolerances you set

Automatic three-way matching checks each invoice line against its purchase order and its receipt. What makes it usable in practice is the tolerance model: you decide what counts as a match, so small, expected variances clear automatically and only genuine discrepancies reach a human.

90%less manual data entry
21,000+customers worldwide
1,500+partners delivering it

Approval: decisions that do not need a licence

Invoices route to the right approvers automatically, with email notifications and a mobile Web Approval Portal — so a decision does not wait for someone to be at their desk, and approvers do not need a Business Central licence to make one.

E-documents: compliance without a side system

Continia eDocuments receives and processes electronic documents over networks such as Peppol, NemHandel and KSeF, so regional e-invoicing obligations are handled in the same place as everything else. See the readiness checklist for what to prepare.

Everything above installs from Microsoft AppSource and updates with your Business Central release. There is no integration project to schedule.
