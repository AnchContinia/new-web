PEPPOL is mandatory sooner than you think — here's your checklist

E-invoicing mandates are landing market by market. The work is less about the deadline than about what has to be true in your system before it arrives.

Continia team14 Apr 20265 min read

E-invoicing mandates are arriving market by market, on different timelines and with different technical requirements. The deadline is the part everyone tracks. The preparation is the part that decides whether the deadline is a formality or a fire.

Check the dates at source

Requirements and go-live dates differ by country and change over time. Treat this as a readiness checklist, not a legal timetable — confirm the specifics for each market you invoice into with that country's tax authority or your local Continia partner.

Who it affects, and when it stops being optional

Two questions decide your exposure: which markets you issue invoices into, and whether you sell to public-sector buyers. Public procurement is typically where mandates land first, and B2B follows on a separate schedule.

The readiness checklist

Master data

  • Every customer and vendor has a valid electronic address, in the identifier scheme the receiving network expects.
  • Your own registration details are complete and match what the authority holds.
  • Tax codes map cleanly to the codes the format requires — this is where most first submissions fail.

Document content

  • Mandatory fields are populated for every document type you send, not just the common ones.
  • Credit notes reference the original invoice in the way the format expects.
  • Attachments, where allowed, travel with the document rather than by email.

Process

  • Someone owns rejections, and rejections are visible the same day.
  • Sent documents are archived in a form you can produce during an audit.
  • Incoming e-documents land in the same queue as everything else, not in a separate inbox.
The organisations that find mandates painless are not the ones that started earliest. They are the ones whose master data was already clean.
