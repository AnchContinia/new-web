The challenge

Documents arrived everywhere: email, post, a shared mailbox, occasionally a photograph. Each channel had its own informal owner, and each owner had a slightly different idea of what “handled” meant.

The result was not chaos so much as uncertainty. Nobody could say with confidence that everything received had been registered — which meant month-end always included a search.

“Every document is captured and matched automatically. We haven't chased a missing invoice in over a year.”Moni · Finance Manager, Calypso Group

What changed

Every channel was pointed at one inbox, and everything in that inbox was captured automatically. Three-way matching then checked each invoice line against its purchase order and receipt, with tolerances the team set themselves, so ordinary variances cleared without a conversation.

Duplicates stopped being a manual check and became something the system refused to create.

The result

Lost and duplicated invoices went to zero and stayed there. The month-end search disappeared with them — not because the team got better at finding documents, but because there was no longer anything to find.
