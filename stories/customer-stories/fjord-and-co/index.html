The challenge

The close was not slow because the numbers were hard. It was slow because the last three days were spent waiting — for approvals sitting in inboxes, for a document someone had filed locally, for confirmation that nothing was outstanding.

“Setup took an afternoon. The time we got back paid for the whole thing inside the first week.”Silke · Head of Accounting, Fjord & Co.

What changed

Because Continia runs inside Business Central, there was nothing to migrate and no second system to learn. The apps installed from Microsoft AppSource into the environment the team already worked in — same interface, same data, same login.

Approvals moved out of email and into a routed workflow with notifications and a mobile portal, so a decision no longer waited for someone to be at a desk.

The result

Month-end close moved two days earlier. The setup cost was an afternoon, and the recovered time covered it inside the first week.
