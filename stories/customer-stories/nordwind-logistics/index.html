The challenge

Volume was the problem long before anyone called it one. A logistics business generates a steady stream of freight, fuel and subcontractor invoices — high in count, low in value, individually trivial and collectively enormous.

The finance team handled it the way most teams do: by absorbing it. Header fields typed in, lines checked against orders by eye, the awkward suppliers handled by the one person who knew their quirks.

“Invoices arrive, get recognized and posted before I've opened my inbox. My team finally does finance, not typing.”Diana · CFO, Nordwind Logistics

What changed

The first move was to stop treating capture as a typing task. Intelligent OCR reads each incoming invoice and drops the recognised values straight into the right Business Central fields, so the team's first contact with a document is a review rather than a transcription.

The second was to make exceptions visible. Invoices that matched cleanly stopped needing attention at all; the ones that did not got a queue of their own.

The result

Manual data entry fell by around 90%. More usefully, the shape of the day changed: the team's time moved from processing to the exceptions and the analysis that had always been squeezed to the end of the month.
