The Continia platform

One platform. Finance that gets its time back.

Three things carry the whole thing. Open one to see what it means in practice.

Continia is not a portal your team logs into. It is pages, actions and fields inside the Business Central they already use, reading the same vendors, dimensions and approval hierarchy. There is nothing to host, nothing to integrate and nothing new to learn — which is why implementation is measured in days rather than months.

Document Capture reads the invoice — vendor, number, amount, lines, order match — and fills the document out. Your team is left with the part that needs a person: the decision. Customers report up to 90% less manual data entry once it is running.

Continia is delivered by 1,500+ local Business Central specialists in 45+ countries — the same partner who knows your chart of accounts, your approval rules and your auditors. You get software from one company and a setup from someone down the road.
